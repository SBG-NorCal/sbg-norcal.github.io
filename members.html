Members events

Private events for active SBG NorCal members and MATS scholarship athletes — open mat rotations, team trips, in-house tournaments, and the occasional surprise.

How this works

These events are powered by a private Google Calendar that's shared with each member individually. If you're signed into Google with the email we have on file, you'll see the full calendar below. If the calendar looks empty or shows a sign-in prompt, you may not be on the share list yet.

{%- comment -%} Fallback panel — shows beneath the iframe. Members will see the calendar populated above; non-members will see an empty / sign-in iframe but the panel below gives them a clear next step. {%- endcomment -%}

Can't see any events?

The calendar above only loads events for members on the share list. If you're an active SBG NorCal member or MATS scholarship athlete and the calendar looks empty:

  1. Make sure you're signed into Google with the email you gave us at signup.
  2. Check that you've been added to the share list. We add new members manually within 24 hours of joining.
  3. Still nothing? Contact the front desk and we'll sort it out:
