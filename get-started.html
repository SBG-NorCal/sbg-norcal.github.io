FAQs

Common questions

Do I need to be in shape first?

No. Our classes are beginner-friendly — most members started with zero experience and zero "fitness base." You set the pace, the coach watches over you, and you'll walk out energized.

What should I wear?

For grappling: anything you can move and sweat in (we'll loan you a Gi). For striking: athletic clothes; we'll loan you gloves and shin guards for your trial.

I'm nervous about sparring / rolling.

Don't be — you won't spar on your first class. Sparring is introduced gradually, only when the coach decides you have the skills and partner-control to do it safely. SBG NorCal's whole approach is "Aliveness within safety."

I have an injury / I'm older. Is this for me?

Yes. Our oldest active member is in their 70s. Our coaches scale everything — let us know what you're working around, and we'll work with it.

What about kids?

We offer youth programs starting at age 3 (Sticky Monkey, parent + toddler). See our Youth Martial Arts page.

Do you offer financial aid?

Yes — through our partner foundation MATS. Learn about MATS scholarships.
