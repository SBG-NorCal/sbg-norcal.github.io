A practical, no-nonsense self-defense intro covering awareness,
positioning, and basic standing and ground defense.

Open to the public, free of charge. No prior martial arts experience
required. Wear comfortable workout clothes.

This event is hosted by SBG NorCal in partnership with **Martial Arts
Training and Scholarship (MATS)**, a 501(c)(3) using martial arts to
build stronger communities.
