A casual community day to kick off the new year.

**11:00 AM — Open Mat (1 hr)**
All belts. Drill, roll, ask questions, work on whatever you've been
meaning to. Bring a gi or no-gi gear — both are happening.

**12:00 PM — Beginner-friendly slow rolls (1 hr)**
First-timers welcome. We'll cover the safety basics, introduce a few
fundamental movements, and pair newcomers with experienced training
partners who roll calmly.

**1:00 PM — Coffee, bagels, hangout (1 hr)**
Stick around, meet the tribe. Coffee is on us — bagels by Boichik. No
sales pitch, just hanging out.

[**RSVP on Partiful**]({{ page.partiful_url }}) so we know how many
bagels to order. If Partiful isn't your thing, just show up — we'll be
here.
