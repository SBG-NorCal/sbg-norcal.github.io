The flagship MATS program. Six weeks. Three days a week, three hours a
day. Young adults train Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, help around the gym, and
work on mindfulness with a certified trainer. We expect commitment to
academics and personal responsibility alongside the training.

To refer a young person, please use the [contact form](/contact/) or
reach out to your school counselor, foster youth caseworker, or local
social services agency.
