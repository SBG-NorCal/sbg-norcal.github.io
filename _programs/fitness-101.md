Starting one's fitness journey can seem like a daunting task. Where do
you start? What exercises are safe at your level? How do you develop a
fitness plan?

To simplify this, our primary focus with Fitness 101 is strength and
mobility. These are the two aspects that we find people are often most
confused about and that also require the most coaching to achieve
improvements safely. All classes are self-paced and beginner-friendly!
Members are encouraged and nudged along by our knowledgeable coaches
who have your goals in mind.

## What you'll learn

- **Compound lifts** — squat, hinge, push, pull
- **Mobility & joint health** — train hard, stay healthy
- **Programming basics** — so you can build your own plan
- **Movement assessment** — start where you actually are
