Open Mat is unstructured training time on our mats. It's where the gym
becomes a community: people drill the techniques they learned in class,
roll with new partners, share what's working, and trade reps with friends
from other schools.

**Rolling and sparring require a coach's approval.** Everyone is welcome to
come in and drill, but before you take live rounds, check in with the coach
on the mat. This keeps newer students safe and makes sure you're matched
with the right partner.

Visiting BJJ practitioners are warmly welcomed — please introduce yourself
to a coach and we'll make sure you have a partner.
