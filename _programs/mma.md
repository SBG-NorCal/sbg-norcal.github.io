SBG NorCal's MMA program is where all the delivery systems come together.
You'll integrate everything from our striking and grappling programs into
the most efficient package for fighting: **stand-up → clinch → ground**.

This isn't a "beginner" class. We ask that you build a foundation in our
Kickboxing and Jiu-Jitsu programs (or have prior experience with coach
approval) before joining MMA. Once you're in, expect deliberate,
supervised, intelligent training — not chaos.

## What you'll learn

- The **Stand-Up / Clinch / Ground** SBG framework
- **Transitions** between ranges under live resistance
- **Wall work** — cage-style clinch and takedowns
- **Fight IQ** — reading opportunities, managing rounds
