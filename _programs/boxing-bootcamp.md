Learn the best striking skills today and combine them with a total-body
workout that will leave you feeling fitter and stronger than ever before!
Our Boxing Bootcamp is part of SBG NorCal's Striking Program and offers
beginners and experienced athletes alike a chance to push themselves with
high-energy workouts and effective self-defense skills.

Boxing Bootcamp is structured for participants to go their own speed.
Challenge yourself and learn to strike with your fists, feet, elbows, and
knees while getting a great workout.

## What you'll learn

- **Conditioning circuits** built around real fight movements
- **Pad work** and partner drills
- **Bag rounds** that build power and gas tank
- **Self-defense fundamentals** you can actually use
