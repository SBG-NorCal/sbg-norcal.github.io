Whether it's for sport or self-defense, striking is a cornerstone for
martial arts. In our Kickboxing / Muay Thai program you'll learn how to
strike with and defend against feet, fists, elbows, and knees. You'll
learn how to control the clinch to deliver blows and execute takedowns.

Our classes are closely supervised and designed with safety in mind.
Members are gradually introduced to the skills they can apply in sparring
drills. This class will get your heart pumping with a whole body workout.
You'll gain competence and confidence as you improve your skills and
fitness!

## What you'll learn

- **Stance & footwork** — the foundation of striking.
- **The full toolbox** — punches, kicks, elbows, knees.
- **Clinch work** — control, breaks, and takedown setups.
- **Defensive structure** — slips, blocks, parries, and exits.
