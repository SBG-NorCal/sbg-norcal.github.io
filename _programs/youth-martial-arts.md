There's simply no better way to give your child a well-rounded foundation
than with our Youth Martial Arts training! At SBG NorCal we have combined
the best aspects of self-defense, physical fitness, mental development,
and social skill-building to help your child thrive.

We offer Youth Martial Arts classes for ages 3-17 years:

- **Sticky Monkey (3-5 yrs)** — a parent and toddler class
- **Explorers Jiu-Jitsu (4-12 yrs)**
- **Tween/Teen Jiu-Jitsu (11-17 yrs)**

## Why parents choose us

- **Real skills, not theater** — jiu-jitsu is the most-tested grappling
  art in the world for a reason.
- **No bullying tolerated** — and the curriculum explicitly teaches kids
  how to handle conflict with calm and confidence.
- **All coaches are background-checked** SBG-certified instructors.
- **MATS scholarships available** for families through Berkeley Unified
  School District.
