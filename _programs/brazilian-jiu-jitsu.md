Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is a core delivery system for anyone interested in
learning a martial art for self-defense. We believe the fundamentals
that work for fighting are also the foundation for the sport. The class
curriculum is systematically presented to ensure that every member is
steadily and safely progressing and developing a well-rounded
understanding of the fundamentals.

You don't have to "be in shape" to enjoy our classes — they are
beginner-friendly. You will walk out of class feeling energized by the
skills you've learned. Come in and start your journey!

## What you'll learn

- **Survival & escape** — the most important skill set for any new grappler.
- **Position before submission** — the SBG hierarchy of control.
- **Stand-up to ground integration** — takedowns, top game, guard work.
- **Live training** — controlled, supervised, intelligent rolling.
