Lily Pagle is SBG NorCal's co-founder and Head Coach. A life-long athlete,
Lily has over 25 years of experience in martial arts. She holds a 3rd degree
black belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and a 2nd degree black belt in Judo.

Lily believes that learning and growth — both physical and mental — can
take place at any age or from any starting point. All that's required is
good coaching and a supportive environment where you can learn skills and
enjoy training.
