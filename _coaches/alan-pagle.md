Alan Pagle has been training and teaching martial arts since 1973. While
his background includes black belts in traditional martial arts, his
collegiate boxing and earning his 2nd degree black belt in Judo are the
biggest influences on his own training and coaching style.

Along with Lily, Alan built SBG NorCal on the conviction that *functional*
martial arts — what works under pressure, with a resisting partner — is
what students deserve.
