About SBG NorCal

Martial arts that work — under pressure, for real life.

We have been part of the East Bay martial arts community and a member of Straight Blast Gym International since 2003. We train the most efficient delivery systems of Stand-Up, Clinch, and Ground.

We're organized as a non-profit under Martial Arts Training and Scholarship (MATS), a 501(c)(3). Every coach graduates from our year-long coach's course, and every coach is a volunteer. That model lets every dollar from membership go back into the gym — and into scholarships for youth who could never otherwise afford to train.

The SBG way

Three things that make us different.

01

Aliveness

Coined by SBG founder Matt Thornton. Every skill is drilled against a resisting partner — because that's the only place the three things that make a technique work can show up.

  • Timing — read the opening, move with it
  • Energy — leverage and position over strength
  • Motion — technique inside real movement
02

The I-Method

Open rolling mostly teaches you whether you won. The middle stage is where skill actually gets built.

  • Introduction — the movement taught clean, without resistance
  • Isolation — a constrained game that keeps the new skill in front of you instead of your best move, with resistance that rises as you succeed and eases when you don't
  • Integration — back into full rolling, where the skill has to find its own moment
03

Street, Sport, Art

One philosophy, three ways in. Most people arrive through one of them and leave with all three.

  • Think Street — grounded in real self-defense, not in fear
  • Train Sport — honest practice against a fully resisting partner
  • Practice the Art — a lifelong pursuit, not a course you finish

Powered by MATS

A gym that gives back — built in.

Every membership at SBG NorCal funds Martial Arts Training and Scholarship (MATS), our 501(c)(3) foundation. MATS uses martial arts to support underserved youth and young adults across the East Bay — through summer camps, scholarship programs, and long-term mentorship.

Founded in 2013 by lifelong martial artists, MATS partners with Berkeley Unified School District, Berkeley Adult School, Alameda County Foster Youth services, and Oakland's Safe Passages to deliver life-changing training to kids who otherwise couldn't access it.
